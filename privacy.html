Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 28, 2026
This policy is a publication-ready draft for a local-first app, but it should still be reviewed against your final production setup and jurisdictional requirements.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Content Machine handles information when you use the mobile app and this legal website. AI Content Machine is designed as a local-first creator workflow app, which means much of your data is intended to remain on your device unless you choose to use optional connected services.

2. Information you provide

Depending on how you use the app, you may provide information such as:

  • account information, such as email address and password used for local account access,
  • creator profile information, such as your creator name, niche, default audience, tone, and workflow preferences,
  • prompts, topics, content ideas, generated scripts, captions, hashtags, shot lists, planner notes, and saved projects,
  • settings related to subscriptions, templates, exports, and optional custom AI endpoints.

3. Local-first storage

AI Content Machine is built to store core working data locally on your device. That can include your profile, app settings, saved content projects, planner assignments, templates, and other workspace data needed to run the app experience.

If you use the app only in its local mode, your creative workspace data is intended to remain on device and not be sent to a central cloud service operated by AI Content Machine.

4. When data may leave your device

Your data may leave the device in limited circumstances, including:

  • when you explicitly export or share content,
  • when you configure and use a custom external AI or local server endpoint,
  • when you use third-party sign-in or identity providers if they are enabled in a future production release,
  • when Apple processes in-app purchase, subscription, or restore-purchase events.

If you connect a custom endpoint, any prompts or content you send through that endpoint will be processed under that provider’s own privacy practices and terms.

5. Subscription and payment information

Subscriptions are offered through Apple’s in-app purchase system. AI Content Machine does not directly collect or store your full payment card details.

  • Apple may provide subscription status, transaction verification, or entitlement-related information needed to unlock Pro features.
  • Billing, refunds, payment processing, taxes, and storefront rules are managed by Apple under Apple’s own terms and privacy policies.

6. How information is used

Information is used to operate and improve the app experience, including to:

  • authenticate you and maintain your local session,
  • save your workspace, profile, and preferences,
  • generate and organize creator workflow output,
  • unlock subscription-based features,
  • support exports, planner workflows, and saved drafts,
  • diagnose technical issues and maintain app reliability where applicable.

7. Data sharing

AI Content Machine does not describe itself as a centralized content cloud service. However, data may be shared with third parties in the following cases:

  • with Apple for app distribution, subscription processing, and related App Store functions,
  • with a third-party endpoint or provider that you choose to connect,
  • where disclosure is required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, security, or the integrity of the service.

8. Data retention

Because the app is local-first, retained data is often determined by what remains on your device unless you delete it. You can remove app content by deleting projects or local app data within the app, or by removing the app from your device, subject to system-level backups and Apple platform behavior.

9. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the app and support its local-first architecture, but no software, device, network, or transmission method is completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and any third-party endpoints you connect.

10. Children’s privacy

AI Content Machine is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law to provide valid consent on their own. Do not use the app if you are not permitted to do so under applicable law.

11. International use

If you use connected services, exports, or third-party endpoints, your information may be processed in jurisdictions other than your own according to the policies of those providers. If your final production setup changes, this section should be reviewed and updated accordingly.

12. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, portability, or complaint. Because much of the app data is stored locally on your device, you may often exercise certain rights directly by editing or deleting your data in the app.

13. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this website with a revised effective date.

14. Contact
